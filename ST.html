ℹ️ How is this computed?
📌 Content count
  • Target no. of content = Monthly Plan rows (category CONTENT) for the selected MONTH × FREQUENCY PER MONTH.
  • Actual no. of content = your Daily Actual rows with category CONTENT (one per “Is this the start date? = Yes”; if not used, one per title). Titles/category containing “proofread” count as Proofread, not Own Content.
  • A content is counted only once it is approved: APPROVAL DATE is filled in, or an Approved file in Approval Requests has the content title in its name. Otherwise it stays in Awaiting approval.
  • Ach % = Own Content ÷ Target.
⏱️ Accuracy · Efficiency · Timeliness (by duration)
  • Based on DURATION PER MIN of approved content only (sum of the minutes of all its Daily Actual rows).
  • Revision mins = minutes of the content for every Rejected file in Approval Requests uploaded that month (matched by title; if no match, the average minutes per content).
  • Accuracy (Quality) % = (Approved mins − Revision mins) ÷ Approved mins, less the Content Errors deduction (1–2 errors = 1%, 3–4 = 2% …).
  • Efficiency % = (Mins w/o revisions − Revision mins) ÷ Mins w/o revisions (not lower than 0%).
  • Timeliness (On-time) % = On-time mins ÷ Own Content mins. On-time = APPROVAL DATE is on or before TARGET DATE.
  • If no Duration per Minute is recorded for the approved content yet, these three temporarily use content count instead.
⚡ Productivity Rate (monitoring only)
  • Productivity = Actual Duration ÷ Adjusted Planned Duration.
  • Actual Duration = sum of DURATION PER MIN of your Daily Actual rows. Content rows count only after the content is approved; other tasks count right away.
  • Adjusted Planned Duration = planned minutes less leave / absent days (480 mins each).
  • Not included in the Total KPI.
🧮 Other KPIs & Total
  • SO Ach, SocMed, Training, S+ and Inventory/Disbursement are read from the “KPI Inputs” sheet (they have no source in the daily logs).
  • Total KPI = Σ (achievement, capped per KPI) × weight — same weights and caps as the ST incentive sheet.
