📈 Overview - Actual Report Summary per Specialized Trainer

Comparison of Actual tasks/durations vs Monthly Plan per trainer.

📝 Computation Guide & Notes
  • Category targets: Monthly Plan frequency per month is counted by category (Content, Task, Others).
  • Count: Target Count comes from Monthly Plan Frequency per Month by category. Each Daily Actual record counts as 1 Actual Count.
  • Minutes: Plan Minutes comes from Monthly Plan frequency × duration per minute. Actual Minutes uses the DURATION PER MIN value from the Daily Actual Google Sheet.
  • Achievement: Category Achievement = Actual Count ÷ Target Count × 100.
  • On Time / Late: A matched actual is On Time only when actual duration is less than the Daily Plan duration; it is Late when actual duration exceeds the plan. Equal durations are neither.
  • Productivity: Actual minutes divided by Monthly Plan target minutes × 100.
  • Efficiency: Daily Plan minutes ÷ Actual minutes × 100, counting only actuals matched to a Daily Plan. 100% = finished exactly on plan; above 100% = faster than plan; below 100% = slower than plan.
