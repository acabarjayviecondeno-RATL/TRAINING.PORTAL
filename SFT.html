🕓 Submission Compliance

Weekly Plan: the whole week (Mon-Sat, less Holiday/Leave) must be planned and submitted on the first working day of the week (if Monday is a holiday, the deadline moves to the next working day). A week with any unplanned day = INCOMPLETE; submitting after the deadline = LATE. You can still submit after the deadline, but it will be flagged. Actual Plan: must be submitted daily (same day as the entry DATE).
